# Jean-Michel Tibi

A one-page profile of Jean-Michel Tibi, the French Formula 1 cameraman and director of photography known in the paddock as "the Legend" (JMT).

The page covers:

- **The job**: his role at Formula One Management, his kit, and a Grand Prix Sunday hour by hour
- **Career**: from Paris motorbike courier (early 1980s) to his 34th F1 season (2025)
- **Shot list**: twelve moments he filmed, from Spa 2000 to Abu Dhabi 2021
- **Jules and Anthoine**: the last pictures he took of Jules Bianchi and Anthoine Hubert
- **Credits**: Sportel awards, BAFTA, his F1 video game appearance, and work beyond F1
- **The memoir**: *30 ans de F1 : Mes histoires secrètes* (City Éditions, 2023)
- **Watch and listen**: the 2026 documentary *The Legend Behind The Lens*, podcasts and interviews
- **Sources**: every public interview and article the page draws on

This is an unofficial profile compiled from public sources. It is not affiliated with Jean-Michel Tibi, Formula 1 or Formula One Management. It contains no photographs, so it carries no image rights.

## Viewing it

The whole site is one static file, `index.html`, with no build step. Open it in a browser, or publish it with GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → deploy from the branch, root folder).

Fonts come from Google Fonts (Archivo, Newsreader, IBM Plex Mono). The page follows the viewer's light or dark system setting and turns off its animation when the viewer has asked for reduced motion.
